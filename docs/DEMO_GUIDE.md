# Hackathon Demo Guide — FollowFlow AI

Follow this 12-step flow for live hackathon presentations and video recordings:

---

## 🎬 12-Step Hackathon Presentation Script

| Step | Action | Feature / Page | Visual / Speech Focus |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Open App | **Dashboard (`/`)** | Show the clean dashboard layout, stats cards, and AI-ranked daily queue. |
| **2** | Highlight Queue | **Dashboard** | Show how leads are ranked by priority score (`96` Critical vs `84` High). |
| **3** | Navigate | **AI Analyzer (`/analyzer`)** | Open the AI Conversation Analyzer from the sidebar. |
| **4** | Load Sample | **AI Analyzer** | Click the **"Load Sample Data (Sarah Johnson)"** button to populate raw transcript. |
| **5** | Analyze | **AI Analyzer** | Click **"Analyze Conversation with AI"**. |
| **6** | Show Results | **AI Analyzer** | Point out the AI extraction result: **Sarah Johnson (Acme Corp)**, **Score 96/100 (Critical)**, Pain Points, Finance Objection, and Next Action. |
| **7** | Add to Queue | **AI Analyzer** | Click **"Add to Follow-Up Queue"** (show success toast). |
| **8** | Navigate | **AI Generator (`/generator`)** | Open the AI Follow-Up Generator. |
| **9** | Configure | **AI Generator** | Select **Email** channel and **Professional** tone. |
| **10** | Generate Copy | **AI Generator** | Click **"Generate Follow-Up"** — show how AI addresses her finance objection and crafts a personalized email. |
| **11** | Show Analytics | **AI Insights (`/insights`)** | Navigate to AI Insights to show pipeline health analytics and objection charts. |
| **12** | Conclude | **Dashboard (`/`)** | Return to Dashboard and conclude presentation. |
