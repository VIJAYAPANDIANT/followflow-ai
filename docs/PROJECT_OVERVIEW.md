## Executive Summary

**FollowFlow AI** is an intelligent AI-powered sales follow-up assistant designed to help sales representatives, account executives, and business development managers avoid losing valuable leads due to missed or delayed follow-ups.

In fast-paced B2B sales environments, representatives communicate with dozens of prospects daily across phone calls, meeting notes, emails, and chat messages. Managing all these interactions manually makes it difficult to track which customer needs a follow-up, when that follow-up should occur, and what specific message or offer will convert the lead.

FollowFlow AI automates this workflow end-to-end. By ingesting raw sales conversations, the system uses **Google Gemini 3.7 Flash AI** to analyze customer intent, extract pain points and objections, detect purchase buying signals, compute a dynamic priority score (`0–100`), recommend the next best action, and draft hyper-personalized follow-up messages for Email, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp.

---

## Core Value Propositions

- **⚡ Zero Lost Revenue**: Prevents high-intent leads from turning into cold prospects due to delayed responses.
- **🎯 Dynamic Priority Ranking**: Automatically tiers leads into *Critical* (`90–100`), *High* (`75–89`), *Medium* (`50–74`), and *Low* (`0–49`) based on intent and timeline.
- **🧠 Automated Intelligence Extraction**: Converts unstructured conversation text into structured JSON sales intelligence in seconds.
- **✉️ Multi-Channel Message Generation**: Drafts context-aware follow-up messages across Email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp in 4 customizable tones.
- **📊 Strategic Pipeline Insights**: Provides real-time analytics on common customer objections, cold lead risk flags, and team close velocity.

---

## Project Objectives

1. Eliminate pipeline leakage caused by manual lead tracking.
2. Provide sales reps with an instant daily action queue ranked by commercial urgency.
3. Reduce time spent writing custom follow-up emails from 15 minutes to under 5 seconds.
4. Deliver actionable insights into pipeline health and customer objections.
