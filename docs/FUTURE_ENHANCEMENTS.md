# Future Roadmap & Enhancements — FollowFlow AI

The following post-hackathon roadmap outlines planned enterprise features and integration expansions for FollowFlow AI:

---

## 🔮 Roadmap & Expansion Features

### 1. CRM Platform Integrations
- **Salesforce & HubSpot**: 2-way automatic sync of lead records, deal stages, and AI priority scores directly into CRM objects.
- **Pipedrive & Zoho CRM**: Auto-create tasks and update pipelines upon AI analysis.

### 2. Email & Calendar Automation
- **Gmail & Outlook Native Integration**: Ingest incoming email threads automatically and send AI-generated follow-up drafts directly from your inbox.
- **Google Calendar & Outlook Calendar**: Auto-schedule recommended follow-up meetings and send calendar invite links in messages.

### 3. Messaging Channel APIs
- **Official WhatsApp Business API**: 1-click direct sending of WhatsApp messages from within the web application.
- **LinkedIn Messaging API**: Automated InMail and message drafting.

### 4. Advanced AI & Analytics Capabilities
- **Real-Time Webhook Notifications**: Push instant browser and mobile alerts when a high-value lead is flagged at risk.
- **Automated Email Sending Workflows**: Trigger scheduled follow-up emails automatically if a prospect does not reply within X days.
- **Predictive Revenue Lead Scoring**: Machine learning model trained on historical closed-won deal data.
- **AI Sales Coaching Assistant**: Real-time feedback for reps on deal negotiation tactics and tone optimization.

### 5. Multi-User & Enterprise Administration
- **Sales Team Workspaces & Roles**: Role-based access control (Sales Rep, Account Executive, Sales Manager, Admin).
- **Manager Dashboard & Revenue Attribution**: Team-wide analytics tracking rep follow-up completion velocity and converted revenue.
